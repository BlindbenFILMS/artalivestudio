What would you like to make?

Start with any picture: a painting, a drawing, a family photo. Everything is made right here in your browser. Free to start, no app to download.

Living picture

Make a picture come alive

Your picture moves, glows and speaks. Print its QR code beside it, and people watch it come alive on the real thing through their phone.

  • Tap a recipe like Windy day or Starry night
  • Add your own voice and sounds
  • Ready in a few minutes

Game

Turn a picture into a game

A slide puzzle, memory match, flashlight hunt, Simon Says and more, made from your picture. Every game can be played by ear.

  • Pick a game, add your picture, share it
  • Nine games ready to go
  • About 1 minute

Magic Postcard

Send a postcard that comes alive

A real postcard in the mail. They scan it, point their phone at it, and the picture starts to move, with your video message playing under it.

  • Pick a place from all 50 states, or use your own photo
  • Record a hello
  • We print and mail it, or you print it yourself

Snow globe

Shake up a snow globe

Put a photo or a video inside a snow globe with your own words on it. They shake their phone and the snow flies.

  • A photo, or a video up to a minute
  • Your words on the glass, a name on the base
  • Pets blink, watch the snow and sneeze

How it works

Three steps, whatever you make

  1. Add your pictureTake a photo or upload one. It stays on your device until you save.
  2. Make it yoursTap what you like. You see it change as you go, and you can undo anything.
  3. Share itSave to a free account for a link and a QR code. A free account publishes 3 pieces.
