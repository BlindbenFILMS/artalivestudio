New · Magic Postcards

Send a postcard that comes alive.

Pick a picture, add your own video and a personal message. We print it and mail it. They scan the QR code, point their phone at the postcard, and the picture starts to move.

LocationsGreetings from all 50 states, from day to a starry night EventsBirthdays, holidays, weddings and every occasion For BusinessesYour brand, your video, your offer, mailed to your list
  1. 1

    Pick a picture

    Choose a place from any of the 50 states (each one turns from day to a starry night), or use your own photo.

  2. 2

    Write and record

    Write your message, then record a hello from where you are. It plays right under the postcard, with your name on a strip above it. Add a second video for the back if you like.

  3. 3

    Say who it's for

    Their name goes on the front. Add their address and who it's from.

  4. 4

    We mail it, or you print it

    We print it on thick card and send it. Or download it, print it yourself and write it by hand. No app needed to watch it.
