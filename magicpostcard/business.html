Magic Postcards

For businesses

A printed postcard that comes alive in your customer's hand. They scan it, the picture moves, and your video plays right under it: a welcome, a tour, an offer, a thank-you. We print and mail it to your whole list, with each person's name on the front.

From The Sea Glass Hotel
A fully produced example

The Sea Glass Hotel

A made-up Asbury Park hotel sends this to last summer's guests in March. The boardwalk comes alive from day to fireworks, a video of happy guests plays under the card, and the back carries the offer and the hotel's logo, which is scannable too.

  • Each guest's name on the front, from your mailing list
  • Your offer on the back: "15% off your summer stay"
  • Your video under the card: a welcome, a tour, happy guests
  • You see who opened it, and guests can send a video or voice reply
  • The sound of the boardwalk: surf, gulls and fireworks

How businesses use them

Every business card can carry your logo, colors and offer; your own photo or one of ours; one video for everyone or a personal one per person; and a link they can scan again any time. Ask us about bulk mailing and custom designs.
