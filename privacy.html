Privacy

Your privacy

Short version: the camera stays on your phone, and we only keep what you choose to send us.

The camera

When you tap Start camera, your phone's camera is used only inside your browser, to find the rectangle you point at. Nothing from the camera is recorded, saved or uploaded. It stops when you leave the page or tap Exit.

The Creator Studio

The studio runs in your browser. Your images and drafts are saved in your own browser on your own device, not on our servers. Clearing your browser's data removes them.

Forms

If you join the waitlist or send us a message, we receive your name, email and what you wrote, through our form provider, Formspree. We use it only to reply to you or to tell you when creator accounts open. We never sell or share it. Ask us any time and we will delete it.

Visitor counts

We may count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not track you across sites. It tells us things like how many people tried the demo, never who they are.

Questions

Write to us through the contact page.
