For galleries

Every wall becomes an experience.

Visitors scan a code by the painting. The art moves, speaks and plays on their phone. No app, no headsets.

Nothing to install

It works in any phone browser. A small label with a code is all you hang.

Accessible by design

Every work is described aloud. The blindness spectrum filter and tactile prints welcome visitors with low vision.

Visitors stay longer

Stories and games turn a quick look into minutes with each work.

We help you launch

We work with you and your artists to bring a whole show to life.

See it in a gallery

The Blind Canvas Project gallery already runs on Art Alive. Open it on your phone and point it at any of the paintings.
