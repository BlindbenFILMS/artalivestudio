For everyone

Point. Watch. Play.

Find the Art Alive code beside a painting, open it on your phone and center your camera on the art. It is free and there is nothing to download.

Hear it

Every painting is described aloud, with its own soundscape.

See it differently

The blindness spectrum filter shows the art through the eyes of people with low vision.

Play it

Each painting has its own game.

Feel it

On phones that vibrate, movement in the painting has its own feel under your finger.

Try it right now

No art nearby? Center any rectangle shape, like a TV, a poster or a sheet of paper, and watch it come alive.
