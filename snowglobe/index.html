Put your photo or video in a snow globe. People shake their phone to make it snow, and your video comes alive.

    Take one now or choose one you have. Videos up to a minute.

  2. 2. Fit it in the globe

    Drag to move it. Pinch or use the slider to zoom.

    This frame shows until someone shakes the globe. Then your video plays from here to the end, and fades back to this frame.
