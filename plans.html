Good to know

What counts as published? A piece with a live link and QR code. A collection doesn't count by itself; each piece inside it counts once.

Drafts are free. Save as many projects to your account as you like. Unpublish a piece any time to free up a space.

Changing or cancelling. Move up, down or cancel whenever you like, through the contact page. If you cancel, nothing is deleted: visitors see your first 3 pieces, and the rest come back if you subscribe again.
