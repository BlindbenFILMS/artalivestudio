Game Maker · new

Turn your art into a game

Pick a game, place a few things on your painting, record your voice and play. Every game talks, beeps and buzzes, so anyone can play it, with or without sight.

1. Your art

A painting, a drawing or a poster. Take a photo and Art Alive helps you line it up, then straightens it. It stays on your device.

5. Your voice

Record each line in your own voice, or just edit the words and a computer voice will read them. Talk like you're showing a friend your painting.

Changes the game’s buttons and the computer voice. Your suggested lines switch too, unless you have edited them.

6. Play and share

Play your game, then save it.

One file that plays in any phone or computer browser. Put it on your website, or send it to friends.

Keeps your game in My projects, so you can open it on any device.

Share it on the wall: press Save to my account, then the blue Publish button at the bottom of the screen. You get a link and a QR code to print beside your art. Visitors scan it and play right on the painting. Showing several pieces? Put up to 50 under one QR code with a collection in My projects.
