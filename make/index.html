Make your own game

Turn your picture into a game.

Pick a game, add a photo or a painting, and share it with a link or a QR code. It takes about a minute, and it’s free.

  1. Pick a gameNine ready to go.
  2. Add your pictureSee it come alive as you go.
  3. Share itA link and a QR code.

Every game can be played by ear. Beeps guide the way, every part has its own note, and a voice welcomes players and reads out your description of the picture. Made with the Blind Canvas Project.

Brainstorm

Games we could make next

Ideas from the Blind Canvas Project games. Each was first made for one painting, and could become a template for any picture. The ones marked Ready you can make today.

Light in the dark

Light up what’s hidden

  • Night light Ready as Flashlight The room goes dark and the phone is a flashlight. Find seven hidden things to a lofi beat. from Midnight Melodies
  • Pieces of me Find broken pieces of a mirror in the dark. Each holds a word about who you are. from The Mirror
  • Light the night Carry a small light across a dark lake to find what’s hidden, each with its own sound. from The Only Light We’ll See
  • Light the glass Rub a finger across dark stained glass and each piece lights up like a sunrise, with a chime. from A New Day
  • Glow it bright Sing or hum: a glowing orb rises and falls with your voice to light things up. from Rhythm of Life

Find it by ear

Sound hunts

  • Sound safari Everyday sounds that help a blind person get around hide in the fog. They get louder as you get close. from Almost Blindness
  • Open road safari City sounds hide between the roads, and you tame the weather too. from Pathways Unveiled
  • Fish for the sisters Point out the fish swimming past so the sisters can see them. from Reflections of Sisterhood
  • Family apples Grow an apple for each person in the family, then find them again through a low-vision filter. from Roots of Kinship

Seeing differently

Play through another person’s eyes

  • See it my way Find hidden words through cataracts, macular degeneration, glaucoma and RP. from Walk Through Fear
  • Catch the words A tunnel of sight follows the eye. Catch the words of a poem as they drift past. from The Visions That We Share
  • Metamorphosis Hold the phone very still and the fog settles so you can see what’s growing inside. from The Gray Zone
  • Show her A window opens onto paintings by other visually impaired artists. from All the Possibilities

Music and memory

Listen, then play it back

  • Light memory Ready as Simon Says The people under the tree each sing a note. Sing their song back by tapping them in order. from Curating Hope
  • Jazz says A musical Simon Says with five instruments living in the painting. from Rhapsody in Blue
  • Night bus The bus rolls through the city while you play a trumpet solo. from The Dark Uncertainty
  • Robot dance party Move your phone and the robot dances to a live funk band. Copy his moves. from Fearless Request
  • Dance in the flowers Tilt to sway and wave to twirl, to a gentle waltz. from All Life’s Beauty

Your voice

Talk, and the painting talks back

  • Say it from the creek A face becomes a puppet that says your answer back. from The Bridge of Perception
  • Healthy self-talk Two heads for the two voices inside: the worry, and the kind answer. from The Scope of Communication
  • What should I paint? Tell the artist an idea and she imagines it. from Flow State
  • Riddles of the lighthouse A talking face by the sea asks riddles about living with blindness. from Lighthouse of Hope

Move your body

Walk, lean and ride

  • Calm walk Red light, green light: walk toward the painting while breathing calmly. from Inner Peace in Outer Chaos
  • Ride the block Be Bill’s eyes. Warn him about things in the road by their sounds. from Pedals of Freedom
  • Ride by feel A buddy calls each curve and you lean into it, by sound alone. from Back of a Harley
  • Sunset joyride Tilt to keep the car in its lane as the boulevard curves. from Joyride
  • Straight as an arrow Drive the highway at dusk as the poles bend over at the top. from Telephone Poles

Weather and calm

Change the sky

  • Blow the storm away Ready as Change the Weather A storm rolls into town. Blow it away so the band can keep playing. from Harmonious Gathering
  • Tides of decision Tilt to give strength to the calm side or the fiery side of a big choice. from Decision Divide
  • Calm the crossing Calm the storms so the people on the bridge can meet in the middle. from Moment of Communion

Caring and together

Help someone else

  • Style the dream Be the stylist: dress three clients from the clothes on the wall. from Haute Couture Dreams
  • Hold on together Link the family’s hands. Every link adds a note to their chord. from Embracing Unity
